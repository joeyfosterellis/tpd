# Rules for this repository

## TPD always

Joey's standing instruction, 25 September 2026: "tpd always".

Every published change to a page in this repository is recorded as a new link in that page's TPD file, named `<Title> — TPD.md`, before the session ends. Append a dated addendum; do not rewrite earlier sections. Record versions with the artifact service's version id and time, the commit, and the file's SHA-256. Quote Joey's words verbatim. Label every part SOURCED, REMEMBERED, GENERATED or UNKNOWN, as in "Read this before making a TPD.md" in Drive. Mark the TPD GENERATED and unaccepted; never mark acceptance from silence.

Keep the Drive copy current: rename the previous Drive copy with its date rather than deleting it, then upload the new one. Nothing is deleted.

## Always cite sources

Joey's standing instruction, 25 September 2026: "remember always cite sources".

Every factual claim on a page or in a TPD names where it came from: a named record in Joey's archive, a URL, or a book with year and page. Web facts name the page and say whether the page was opened or only a search snippet was read. Chat replies that draw on web search end with a Sources list of links. A claim with no source is labelled UNKNOWN as to source, not left bare. Joey's own words are quoted verbatim and dated; they are not paraphrased into facts.

## The equation is Joey's

Joey's instruction, 26 September 2026: "remember:" followed by his statement of the equation.

Before writing anything about 0 → 7 → 0′, read `The Equation — Joey's words.md`. Quote from it; do not paraphrase it into a definition. Where a page or TPD explains the equation, the words are his and are credited as his.
