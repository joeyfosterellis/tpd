## 6. Competing readings and counterevidence

1. **The octave proves too little or too much.** High Do is a different pitch, 523.25 Hz against 261.63 Hz. The page uses it to show 0′ ≠ 0, but the difference there is measurable in the state itself, not only in its history. Joey's equation claims the second zero differs by history even when it looks the same. The octave is a weaker illustration than it appears.

2. **The tangram counter asserts history rather than showing it.** The rebuilt square is geometrically identical to the first. Only the page's counter (0, 0′, 0″) records that it has been a boat. That is bookkeeping added by the page. A viewer who did not see the boat cannot tell 0′ from 0. This supports the equation's premise (history is not visible in the state) but it also means the demonstration depends on a record, not on the object.

3. **The pinball blueprint is the strongest counter-case in the archive.** Its zero-drift rule makes return exact by construction. In that system 0′ = 0 for every animated value. History survives only in the ball's route as a single object and in the documents. The theory as stated on the page says 0′ ≠ 0 always. The blueprint says it can be engineered away. Either the theory means "history is never in the state and must be carried elsewhere", or the blueprint is a counterexample. This TPD does not decide.

4. **7 and 0′ are one landing on the page and two states in the equation.** The page labels the eighth step "7 · 0′" and says the scale takes seven arrows to come round. Joey's chain has 7 as a state and T0 as an eighth arrow to 0′. The page's reconciliation was made by Claude for the scale case and is not in Joey's text.

5. **A simpler reading of the page.** It is a set of clue diagrams for a scavenger hunt with children. The equation section may be doing more than the page needs. The rest of the page works without it.

### 19.2 The seven colours, recomputed (GENERATED method, SOURCED constants)

Until v9 the seven note colours were chosen by eye (v1) and adjusted by eye (v4). From v10 each is the colour of its wavelength, computed:

1. Wavelength → CIE 1931 XYZ, using the 2° standard observer's colour-matching functions at that wavelength (table values, listed below).
2. XYZ → linear sRGB with the standard D65 matrix (3.2406, −1.5372, −0.4986 / −0.9689, 1.8758, 0.0415 / 0.0557, −0.2040, 1.0570).
3. Negative channels clipped to 0 (a spectral colour lies outside the sRGB gamut; clipping is the usual convention for spectrum strips).
4. Scaled so the largest channel is 1 (full brightness), then sRGB gamma-encoded.
