      About

      The drafts

      Every passage in this game is quoted from Tootsie in the Desert by Joey Foster Ellis. All versions are treated as one story. Where drafts disagree (Syrian or Lebanese, pomegranate or pineapple, "pure translation" or "recitation"), the game keeps every version and shows them side by side.

      Drafts read for this game:

      • Tootsie V1 (October 2021): prologue, Chapter 1 "Absolute Faith", synopsis
      • The May 2023 manuscript
      • Fifteen named drafts: khakis, hydrant, coat, polaris, habibi, desalination, lavender, musalla, argan, flamingo, mirror, gomorrah, cigarette, loofah, rayon
      • An Apple Note of 27 March 2013: "Chinese whispers in French is Arabic whispers"
