# A clue, not a meaning

GENERATED. Drafted by Claude on 26 September 2026 at Joey's ask: "please put all this in archive and then write a post of a clue to find their own meaning". Unaccepted until Joey posts it, rewrites it, or says no. The words below the line are the post.

---

Seven notes. Seven pieces. Seven colours. You have seen the page. You want to know what it means.

Here is the only clue I will give you.

Somewhere on the page there are two zeros, and they are not the same. Nobody will tell you why. The person who drew the page said it this way:

> The numbers show where something is. The arrows show what happened to it. The final zero shows that returning is not the same as never having left.
>
> Joey Foster Ellis, 25 September 2026

So do not look at the numbers. Look at the arrows.

Then go and find one thing in your own house that has come back to where it started. A cup that was washed. A key that was lost. A chair you sat in yesterday. Hold it. Ask what happened to it between leaving and returning.

That is the meaning. Not mine. Not Joey's. Yours, because the history is yours.

The Egyptians had a sign for this. It is a beetle, and it is also the verb "to become". Find it. Then find the sign that is a ring with a knot. Decide which of the two is your morning.

When you know, don't tell us what it means. Tell us what happened.

The ring with a knot is the *shen* ring (Gardiner V9), "encircling", per https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shen_ring, search snippet only.
