# A clue

Eight marks on a page:

**0 1 2 3 4 5 6 7**

Then a ninth that looks like the first.

Don't read the marks. Nobody hid anything in them. Seven is not heavier than three.

Read the gaps.

Pick any two neighbours. Ask what had to happen for the first to become the second. Heat? Breath? A word said out loud? A year? Whatever you answer is the clue. It's yours. Nobody else's answer will fit your gap, and yours won't fit theirs.

Now try to walk one gap backwards. Some let you. Some don't. Notice which.

When you reach the ninth mark, look closely. It is not the first mark. It has been somewhere.

That's the whole game. The marks show where a thing is. The gaps show what happened to it. The meaning was never in the marks. It's in what you say happened between them, and you are the only one who can say it.

Start anywhere. Start where you end.
