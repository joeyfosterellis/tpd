# A clue, not an answer

I'm not going to tell you what this means. I'm going to give you a clue.

0 → 1 → 2 → 3 → 4 → 5 → 6 → 7 → 0'

The numbers are empty on purpose. A 7 isn't a prize and a 3 isn't a punishment. They wait for you to bring something to them.

**Clue #1: look between the numbers.**

Pick any gap, say 3 → 4. Now think back to the last time you really changed. What pushed you across? A hard conversation? A long walk? Something you lost? Something you finally understood?

Whatever it was, that's your arrow. Your meaning isn't sitting in the numbers. It's in whatever carries you from one to the next.

**Clue #2: nobody else's arrows look like yours.**

Mine might be music. Yours might be the sea, your kids, a notebook, silence. That's the point. You can't borrow meaning. You notice what moves you.

**Clue #3: 0' is not 0.**

Ever gone back somewhere from years ago and found it smaller, or quieter, or kinder than you remembered? The place didn't change. The loop did its work on you.
