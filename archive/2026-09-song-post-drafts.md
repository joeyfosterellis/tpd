## Draft 2 (longer, gentler)

To anyone who's feeling anxious, overwhelmed or stuck right now, this is for you.

I've been thinking about a simple idea:

0 → 1 → 2 → 3 → 4 → 5 → 6 → 7 → 0'

The numbers are just states, places we pass through. None of them is more important than another, and none of them defines you.

What really matters are the arrows, the things that move us from one state to the next. Sometimes it's something big, but usually it's small: one breath, one hour, one kind word, one night's sleep. Healing, learning and even loss are arrows too.

So if you're sitting at a 3 right now, you don't have to leap to 7. You only need the next arrow. 3 → 4. That's all.

And the loop doesn't send you back to where you started. It ends at 0', which looks familiar but holds everything you've been through. Coming back around isn't failure. It's growth.

I wrote a song from this idea, and I hope it brings you some calm today. Take a breath, press play and let it carry you to the next arrow. 💛
